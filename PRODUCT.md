# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Office managers, facility and property managers, and owners of professional practices (law firms, mortgage and finance firms, design studios, schools) in Toronto and Vaughan. Most are replacing a cleaner that needed constant reminders. They visit between other tasks, often on a desktop at work, sometimes on a phone. The job: find a reliable recurring janitorial provider and hand the problem off for good.

## Product Purpose
The homepage for Luminari Cleaning, a recurring commercial janitorial company. It has to earn enough trust for a manager to request a walkthrough or build a cleaning plan. Success is a qualified walkthrough request from a commercial site, not traffic.

## Brand Personality
Calm, considered, dependable. The reader should feel "this is handled now": quiet confidence rather than hype. Voice is plain, short and specific ("A clear scope. A familiar team."), never salesy. Emotional goal: relief.

## Anti-references
- Generic cleaning-company sites: stock photos of people in gloves, sparkle and bubble icons, bright blue and green palettes, "squeaky clean" and "spotless guaranteed" clichés.
- Anything that shouts: countdowns, discount banners, exclamation-point copy.

## Design Principles
1. **Practice what you preach.** A cleaning company's site must itself be orderly: consistent spacing, nothing out of place, no clutter.
2. **Show the handoff.** Make the process, the point of contact and the paper trail visible; that is the real product.
3. **One clear next step.** Each view asks for a single main action; everything else is secondary.
4. **Quiet motion.** Motion confirms and guides; it never competes with the message or moves on its own for long.
5. **Proof over promises.** Named clients, insurance and WSIB, and the owner's name do the persuading, not adjectives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: AA contrast on every pairing, full keyboard access with visible focus, 44px minimum targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, and a pause control for any content that moves for more than 5 seconds.
